v1.0.0
Cultural Fidelity Priority

This release delivers Sangeet's baseline Phase 1 ML pipelines, fully tuned to preserve playback vocal signatures and ornaments (meend, gamak) without Western compression flattening.

Key Improvements

  • Integrated high-performance **DeepFilterNet 3** denoise model to eliminate broadband tape hiss.
  • Custom impulsive click & crackle removal stage optimized against sitar, sarod, and tabla transients.
  • Bandwidth extension via **NVSR** to cautiously restore high-frequency detail up to 48 kHz without introducing digital artifacts.
  • Fully native AVAudioEngine mastering chain supporting integrated LUFS and true-peak normalization.

Security & Compliance

  • Cryptographically signed binaries conforming to strict macOS Hardened Runtime configurations.
  • Full license auditing ensuring 100% EULA-compliant third-party models and libraries.
